GYPSo — Support

GYPSo (Guess Your Playlist Song) is a music game that turns your own Apple Music playlists into a listening quiz.

Getting started

What do I need?
An active Apple Music subscription and at least one playlist with a handful of tracks that have a release year in their metadata.
How do I play?
Connect to Apple Music, pick a playlist and a session length (5, 10 or 15 tracks), and answer three things about each track: the year, the title and the performer. The faster you commit a round, the bigger the speed bonus.
What makes a good playlist?
A large one — dozens to hundreds of tracks you know, across many artists and decades, with reliable release years in the metadata. That keeps the wrong answers plausible and the year a real guess. A short playlist from a single era is usually too easy; playlists built from compilations, live albums or remasters tend to have unreliable years.
Why is a playlist “not enough tracks”?
A session needs at least as many tracks with a release year as the round count you chose. Try a larger playlist or a shorter session.
Why doesn’t the track start at 0:00?
By default each round starts a little way into the track — past most intros — rather than from the beginning, and keeps playing until you log your answers (or the track ends). This is deliberate, not a bug — turn on Start songs from the beginning in Settings if you'd rather it always start at 0:00.
No sound?
Check that Apple Music plays normally, the device isn’t on silent for media, and GYPSo has Media & Apple Music permission in iOS Settings → GYPSo.

Privacy

GYPSo runs entirely on your device — no account, no analytics, no server. See the Privacy Policy.
