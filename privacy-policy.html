Privacy Policy — GYPSo (Guess Your Playlist Song)

Last updated: 30 August 2026

GYPSo is a single-player music game that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what it does and does not do with your information.

What GYPSo accesses

  • Your Apple Music library. With your permission, GYPSo reads the playlists and tracks in your Apple Music library so it can build each game and draw the answer choices. It plays those tracks through Apple Music.
  • On-device storage. Your game scores and your settings (chosen playlist, number of rounds, theme, and similar preferences) are saved locally on your device so the app remembers them between sessions.

What GYPSo does not do

  • There is no GYPSo account and no sign-up.
  • There is no analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash reporting.
  • There is no server. GYPSo does not transmit your library, your scores, your device identifiers, or anything else about you to the developer or to any third party.
  • GYPSo does not use the Advertising Identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

Data retention and deletion

Everything GYPSo stores is kept only on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it. You can also clear your saved highscores at any time from Settings → Delete all highscores.

Apple Music

GYPSo uses Apple’s MusicKit framework to access your library and play music. Your use of Apple Music is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. GYPSo is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple Inc.

Children

GYPSo does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
